GYSMALFIT Bot – Privacy Policy

GYSMALFIT Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account @gysmalfit. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

The bot uses Google Drive with the drive.file permission. It can only see, create and delete the files it uploads itself (backup copies of videos and photos scheduled for @gysmalfit). It cannot read any other files in the Drive.

How data is used

Backup files are kept for about four months after posting and then deleted automatically. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
